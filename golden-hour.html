Golden Hour Magic: Making Art with Natural Light

Golden hour is the short period just after sunrise and before sunset. During this time the sun creates warm, soft light that enhances skin tones, reduces harsh shadows, and adds depth to every image.

At Craftline Pictures, we carefully schedule outdoor shoots around golden hour to create cinematic photographs. Whether it's a pre-wedding session in Munnar, a family portrait in Theni, or a travel shoot in the hills, this natural light transforms every frame into timeless artwork.

Tips for your next golden hour shoot:

  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before sunset.
  • Wear soft pastel or earthy colors.
  • Choose open locations with natural scenery.
  • Relax and enjoy the moment—the best candid photos come naturally.
